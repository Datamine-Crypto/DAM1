# said of it on that node, the whole count as it was. tom has 3 balls is tom -> {has} -> ball(quantity: 3); one of
# the balls is red adds color: red to the ball, the whole count as it was. Each scene tells a count, then singles one
# out, then asks the whole count, the fact, and the owner.
