# Half and double: half of a number is the number divided by two, and double or twice a number is the
# number times two, as the words say.
# The turns ask one half or double each. Each scene tells what two or three people have, then asks half,
# double and twice of the numbers and of each one's count, each count, a total, and who has more.
turn: what is half of 10? => | 5
turn: what is double 6? => | 12
turn: what is twice 4? => | 8
turn: what is half of 18? => | 9
