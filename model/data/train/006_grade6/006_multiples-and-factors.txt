# Multiples and factors: a number is a multiple of another when the other divides it with nothing left
# over, and then the other is a factor of it.
# The turns ask one multiple or factor each. Each scene tells what three people have, then asks multiples
# and factors of the numbers, each count, a total, and who has more or fewer.
turn: is 12 a multiple of 4? => | yes
turn: is 15 a multiple of 4? => | no
turn: is 3 a factor of 18? => | yes
turn: is 7 a factor of 20? => | no
tom has 12 apples. ann has 4 apples. sam has 18 pens. => tom -> {has} -> apple:quantity(12); {number:value(12)}; ann -> {has} -> apple:quantity(4); {number:value(4)}; sam -> {has} -> pen:quantity(18); {number:value(18)} | is 12 a multiple of 4? = yes; is 15 a multiple of 4? = no; is 3 a factor of 18? = yes; is 7 a factor of 20? = no; is 4 a factor of 12? = yes; is 18 a multiple of 4? = no; is 18 a multiple of 3? = yes; how many apples does tom have? = 12; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 16; how many pens does sam have? = 18; who has more apples? = tom
