# statement and in the question that asks it back.
# Each scene tells three or four things with an before their names, then asks each back with an, with
# the or without an article, what has a count, yes and no, and a thing the scene never told. A scene
# made of traced lines keeps their traces one after another, with null for the untraced sentences.
