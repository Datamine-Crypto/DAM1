# A tale opens with once upon a time, with a comma after it or none. The words tell nothing of the
# world: the sentence is read as begun at the word after them. Held out lines tell of things the
# learned lines never tell of.
once upon a time, there was a little car named beep. => car -> {is} -> little | what was the car named? = beep
once upon a time, there was a little car named beep. => car -> {is} -> little | is the car little? = yes
once upon a time there was a big dog called rex. => dog -> {is} -> big | what was the dog called? = rex
once upon a time, there was a small boat named luna. => boat -> {is} -> small | what was the boat named? = luna
once upon a time, there was a big bear called bruno. => bear -> {is} -> big | is the bear big? = yes
once upon a time there was a little train named pip. => train -> {is} -> little | what was the train named? = pip
once upon a time, there was a small cat called mimi. => cat -> {is} -> small | is the cat big? = no
once upon a time, a king lived in a castle. => | where did the king live? = castle
once upon a time a girl lived in a village. => | who lived in the village? = girl
once upon a time, tom had a red kite. => tom -> kite -> {is} -> {color} -> red | what color is the kite? = red
once upon a time, there was a big ship named nora. => ship -> {is} -> big | what was the ship named? = nora
