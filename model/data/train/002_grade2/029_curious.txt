# What the network is curious about: asked what it is curious about or interested in, it names the
# newest thing it was told of, the one it knows least of and wants to hear more about. Held out lines
# tell of things the learned lines never tell of.
the car is red. => | what are you curious about? = car
tom has a dog. => | what are you curious about? = dog
ann went to the park. => | what are you interested in? = park
my cat is called luna. => | what are you curious about? = cat
the box is in the garden. sam has a kite. => | what are you curious about? = kite
the door is open. => | what are you interested in? = door
