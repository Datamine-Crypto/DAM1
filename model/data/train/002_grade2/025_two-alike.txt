# Two things of one name told apart by a quality: the red ball is in the box and the blue ball is in
# the bag are two balls, since a ball that is red is not the blue one. A question names the one it
# asks of by its quality. Held out lines use things, qualities and places the learned lines never use.

seed: 18-qualities
the red ball is in the box. the blue ball is in the bag. => ball -> {in} -> box; ball -> {in} -> bag | where is the red ball? = box; where is the blue ball? = bag; what is in the bag? = ball; what is in the box? = ball
