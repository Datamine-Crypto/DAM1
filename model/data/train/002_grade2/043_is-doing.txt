# Is and a going form of a verb say what someone does now: anna is reading a book is anna reads a
# book, and the dog is sleeping is the dog's activity. Held out lines use doers and things the
# learned lines never use.
anna is reading a book. => anna -> {read} -> book | what does anna read? = book
the dog is sleeping. => dog -> {activity} -> sleep | what does the dog do? = sleep
tom is painting a picture. => tom -> {paint} -> picture | who paints the picture? = tom
mia is eating an apple. => mia -> {eat} -> apple | what does mia eat? = apple
the baby is crying. => baby -> {activity} -> cry | who cries? = baby
ben is building a house. => ben -> {build} -> house | what does ben build? = house
the frog is jumping. => frog -> {activity} -> jump | what does the frog do? = jump
