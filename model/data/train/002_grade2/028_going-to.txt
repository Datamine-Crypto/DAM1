# Going somewhere said with is and a going word: tom is going to rome moves tom as tom went to rome
# does, and where is tom going asks where he is. Held out lines use people, going words and places the
# learned lines never use together.
tom is going to rome. => | where is tom going? = rome; where is tom? = rome
we are going to paris. => | where are we going? = paris
ann is walking to the park. sam is driving to the farm. => | where is ann? = park; where is sam? = farm; who is in the park? = ann
i am going to the shop. => | where am i going? = shop
lucy is flying to madrid. ben is going to the zoo. => | where is lucy going? = madrid; where is ben? = zoo; who is in the zoo? = ben
