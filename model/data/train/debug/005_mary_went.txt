# mary went to the kitchen: went adds a movement relation by its base form with its tense,
# {children add: go type: movement time: past}; to and the point at nothing; the kitchen is the value under go.
# where is mary reads the newest place under a movement or a location.
mary went to the kitchen. => mary -> {go}:time(past) -> kitchen:the(true) | where is mary? = kitchen
