# Comparing fractions: a whole cut into fewer equal parts has bigger parts, so a half is bigger than a
# third, and a third bigger than a quarter; the smaller part is the one cut into more.
# Each scene tells three people who each have a part, then asks which of two parts is bigger or smaller
# (both ways round), who has each part, what each has, yes and no, and a part the scene never gave.
# One turn of each kind stays a chat turn.
