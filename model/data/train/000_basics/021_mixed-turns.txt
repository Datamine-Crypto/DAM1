# Mixed turns: a chat asks about the world, then tells a story, then asks about the story, then asks about
# the world again, all on one stack. Each chain starts with a turn and goes on with then lines, so the
# network learns that a statement after an answered question is written like any other and that a
# question after a story reads the story first. The world questions read the seeds every chat starts from.
seed: 21-verb-forms
seed: 30-geography
seed: 80-words
seed: 90-everyday
seed: 18-qualities
turn: where is japan? => | asia
then: tom has a car. => tom -> {has} -> car | who has the car? = tom; what does tom have? = car
then: turn: where is france? => | europe
then: tom gave the car to ann. => ann -> {has} -> car; tom -> {has} -> car -> {time} -> {past} | who has the car? = ann; who gave the car? = tom
turn: what is the capital of france? => | paris
then: ann has a red hat. => ann -> {has} -> hat -> {red} | what color is the hat? = red; who has the hat? = ann
then: turn: what is the opposite of hot? => | cold
then: the hat is in the box. => hat -> {in} -> box | where is the hat? = box; what is in the box? = hat
turn: where is italy? => | europe
