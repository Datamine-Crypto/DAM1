# Yes, no and nothing. A yes or no question looks a fact up and checks whether the thing it names is
# among what came back; a look-up that finds nothing answers nothing.
seed: 90-everyday
seed: 18-qualities
tom has a car. the car is red. => tom -> {has} -> car -> {is} -> red | does tom have a car? = yes; is the car red? = yes; is the car blue? = no; does tom have a bike? = no
the cat is in the box. => cat -> {in} -> box | is the cat in the box? = yes; is the cat in the bag? = no; what is in the bag? = {nothing}
ann has a dog. => ann -> {has} -> dog | does ann have a dog? = yes; does ann have a cat? = no; what does ann have? = dog
