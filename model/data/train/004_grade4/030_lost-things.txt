# A thing someone lost is put where they stand, and since the story never told that place the
# thing carries the tag assumed, so a person sees it is a guess. Held out lines use people and
# things the learned lines never use.
john is in the garage. he lost his car. => garage -> car -> {assumed} | where is john? = garage
anna is in the park. she lost her hat. => park -> hat -> {assumed} | where is the hat? = park
tom is in the kitchen. tom lost the key. => kitchen -> key -> {assumed} | where is the key? = kitchen
mia is in the garden. she is sad because she lost her ball. => garden -> ball -> {assumed} | how does mia feel? = sad
ben is in the office. he lost his pen. => office -> pen -> {assumed} | where is ben? = office
