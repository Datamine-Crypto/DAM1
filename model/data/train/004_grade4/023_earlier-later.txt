# Earlier and later: on the same day a smaller hour comes before a bigger one, so of two times the
# earlier is the one with the smaller hour and the later the one with the bigger hour.
# Each scene tells when two or three things start, then asks which of many pairs of times is earlier or
# later (the scene's own hours among them), when each thing starts, which starts first, and a thing the
# scene never told. One turn of each kind stays a chat turn.
