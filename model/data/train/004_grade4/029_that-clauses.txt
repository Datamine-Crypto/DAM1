# What a thing is and what it does in one sentence: a clock is a device that measures time. The word
# that, said after what the thing is, sends the reading back to the thing, so the verb after it is said
# of the clock and not of the device. The verbs are ones few other lessons use, so they are read by
# their ending and their place, and held out lines use things and verbs the learned lines never use.
seed: 90-everyday
a clock is a device that measures time. a scale is a tool that weighs fruit. => clock -> {is} -> device; clock -> {measure} -> time; scale -> {is} -> tool; scale -> {weigh} -> fruit | what measures time? = clock; what is a clock? = device; what does a clock measure? = time; what weighs fruit? = scale; what is a scale? = tool; what does a scale weigh? = fruit
