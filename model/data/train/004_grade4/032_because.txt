# The clause after because tells why: the thing the first clause told of holds the thing the second
# clause told of under because, and why asks for it. Held out lines use people and things the learned
# lines never use.
john is in the garage. he is sad because he lost his car. => john -> {because} -> car | why is john sad? = car
grandma is mad because the bucket fell. => grandma -> {because} -> bucket | why is grandma mad? = bucket
the dog is in the house because the cat is in the garden. => dog -> {because} -> cat | where is the cat? = garden
