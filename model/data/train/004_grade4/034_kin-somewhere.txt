# A person named only by what they are to someone, my sister, becomes a thing of the story when the
# sentence says where they are or where they went, so they can be found there. Held out lines use
# people and places the learned lines never use.
my sister lives in berlin. => berlin -> sister | where does my sister live? = berlin
tom's brother works in london. => london -> brother | where does tom's brother work? = london
my aunt is in the garden. => garden -> aunt | where is my aunt? = garden
ann's cousin went to the park. => park -> cousin | where is ann's cousin? = park
my father sat on the bench. => bench -> father | where is my father? = bench
