# Carrying a sum on: a chat asks a sum, then types a sign and a number alone, which works on the answer
# just said, as a person keeps a calculation going. Each chain starts with a sum as a turn and goes on
# with then turns of one sign and one number, so the network learns that a bare sign continues from the
# last number it answered.
