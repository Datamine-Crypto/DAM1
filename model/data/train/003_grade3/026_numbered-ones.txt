# Things of one kind told apart by their place in an order: the first cup and the second cup are two
# cups, each with what it has and what it is, beside a count of them all. A question that names the
# place asks of that one. Held out lines use kinds, places and things the learned lines never join.
there are three cups. the second cup has the key. the first cup has a coin. => | what does the second cup have? = key; what does the first cup have? = coin; how many cups are there? = 3
the first box is red. the second box is blue. => | what color is the second box? = blue; what color is the first box? = red
there are two bags. the first bag has a pen. the second bag has a map. => | what does the first bag have? = pen; what does the second bag have? = map; how many bags are there? = 2
the third car is green. the first car is white. => | what color is the third car? = green; what color is the first car? = white
the second jar has a ring. the third jar has a stone. => | what does the third jar have? = stone; what does the second jar have? = ring
