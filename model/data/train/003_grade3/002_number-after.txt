# The number after and before: whole numbers count up by one, so the number after a number is one more
# and the number before it is one less.
# The turns ask one number each. Each scene tells what two or three people have, then asks the numbers
# after and before, each count, a total, how many more one has, and who has more or fewer.
turn: what number comes after 9? =>  | 10
turn: what number comes before 20? =>  | 19
turn: what number comes after 99? =>  | 100
turn: what number comes before 1? =>  | 0
tom has 9 apples. ann has 20 apples. sam has 99 pens. => tom -> {has} -> apple:quantity(9); {number:value(9)}; ann -> {has} -> apple:quantity(20); {number:value(20)}; sam -> {has} -> pen:quantity(99); {number:value(99)} | what number comes after 9? = 10; what number comes before 20? = 19; what number comes after 99? = 100; what number comes before 1? = 0; how many apples does tom have? = 9; how many apples does ann have? = 20; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 29; what number comes after 20? = 21; what number comes before 9? = 8; how many pens does sam have? = 99; who has more apples? = ann
kim has 15 cards. joe has 30 cards. lily has 7 stamps. => kim -> {has} -> card:quantity(15); {number:value(15)}; joe -> {has} -> card:quantity(30); {number:value(30)}; lily -> {has} -> stamp:quantity(7); {number:value(7)} | what number comes after 15? = 16; what number comes before 30? = 29; what number comes after 7? = 8; what number comes before 15? = 14; how many cards does kim have? = 15; how many cards do kim and joe have? = 45; how many more cards does joe have than kim? = 15; how many stamps does lily have? = 7; who has fewer cards? = kim
