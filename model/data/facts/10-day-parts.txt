# The parts of a day a story names: yesterday, then today with its morning, afternoon and evening in it, then tomorrow,
# each day the successor of the one before it and each part of today the successor of the part before it, so a fact
# told for the afternoon comes after one told for the morning whatever order the story tells them in.
the successor of yesterday is today.
the successor of today is tomorrow.
the morning is in today.
the afternoon is in today.
the evening is in today.
the successor of morning is afternoon.
the successor of afternoon is evening.
