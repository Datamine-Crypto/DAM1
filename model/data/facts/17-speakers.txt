# says for them stand for them: i, me, my, mine and myself are the user, and you, your and yours are
# the assistant, so a fact told with i is written on the user and a question with you asks about the
# assistant.

the self of me is user.
the self of my is user.
the self of mine is user.
the self of myself is user.
the self of you is assistant.
the self of your is assistant.
