# Comparisons told as orders: a comparative and its opposite are each other's inverse, told both ways,
# so a chain told with the one is the same chain read from the other end with the other, and the
# superlative is the top of the chain its comparative draws.
the opposite of taller is shorter.
the opposite of bigger is smaller.
the opposite of older is younger.
the opposite of faster is slower.
the opposite of higher is lower.
the opposite of hotter is colder.
the opposite of heavier is lighter.

the top of bigger is biggest.
the top of older is oldest.
the top of higher is highest.
the top of denser is densest.
the top of heavier is heaviest.
the top of lighter is lightest.

the top of shorter is shortest.
