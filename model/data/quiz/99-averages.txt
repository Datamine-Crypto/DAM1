# Averages: the average of some numbers is their sum shared equally among them, so it is the numbers
# added and divided by how many numbers there are.
# The traced turns stay single lessons. Each scene tells what two or three people have, then asks many
# averages and means, each count, sums, who has the most or more, how many more, and a person the scene
# never told.
turn: what is the average of 4, 6 and 8? {eom} => | 6
turn: what is the average of 10 and 20? {eom} => | 15
turn: what is the mean of 2, 3, 4 and 7? {eom} => | 4
