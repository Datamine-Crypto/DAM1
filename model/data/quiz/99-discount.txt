# Discounts: a price with some percent off costs that many hundredths of the price less, so the price
# after the discount is the price less its percent.
# The traced turn stays a single lesson. Each scene tells two or three prices, then asks many prices
# after a discount (the scene's own prices among them), each price, a sum, how much more, which costs
# more, and a thing the scene never told.
turn: how much is 20 dollars after 10 percent off? {eom} => | 18
the coat costs 20 dollars. the bag costs 50 dollars. the lamp costs 80 dollars. => coat is 20; bag is 50; lamp is 80; 20 = 20; 50 = 50; 80 = 80 | how much is 20 dollars after 10 percent off? = 18; how much is 50 dollars after 20 percent off? = 40; how much is 80 dollars after 25% off? = 60; how much is 10 dollars after 50 percent off? = 5; how much is 50 dollars after 10 percent off? = 45; how much is 80 dollars after 50 percent off? = 40; how much is the coat? = 20; how much does the lamp cost? = 80; how much do the coat and the bag cost in total? = 70; how much more does the lamp cost than the bag? = 30; what costs more, the coat or the bag? = bag; how much is the hat? = nothing
