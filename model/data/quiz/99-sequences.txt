# Guessing a run of numbers: what comes next is the last number and the same step again, or the
# same factor again; what is missing between two numbers is the one halfway, and in a longer run
# it fills the one step twice as wide as the others. Read by the words missing and next. A second
# run in the same line would make the missing or the next number unclear, so each scene tells one
# run and asks many things about it: the next or missing number, its ends, its size and its sum.
1, 3. => 1 = 1; 3 = 3 | what is the missing number? = 2; what number is missing? = 2; what is the first number? = 1; what is the last number? = 3; what is the largest number? = 3; what is the smallest number? = 1; how many numbers are there? = 2; what is the sum of the numbers? = 4
10, 30. => 10 = 10; 30 = 30 | what is the missing number? = 20; what number is missing? = 20; what is the first number? = 10; what is the last number? = 30; what is the largest number? = 30; what is the smallest number? = 10; how many numbers are there? = 2; what is the sum of the numbers? = 40
