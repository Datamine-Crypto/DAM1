# Sharing: a count shared equally by some people gives each of them the count divided by how many they
# are, asked with how many each gets. A count that starts the sentence (twenty pens are shared by four
# students) is not yet read by the reader and is left out. The turns stay as single chat turns; each
# scene tells two people sharing different things with different groups, then asks what each one of
# every group gets, how many share, who shares, and how much is shared.
