# The number between: whole numbers come in order, so the number between two numbers one apart from
# a middle one is that middle number, the one after the smaller and before the bigger.
# The turns ask one number each. Each scene tells what two or three people have, two counts one apart
# from a middle number, then asks the numbers between, each count, a total, how many more, and who has more.
turn: what number is between 6 and 8? {eom} => | 7
turn: what number comes between 19 and 21? {eom} => | 20
turn: what number is between 100 and 98? {eom} => | 99
turn: which number is between 3 and 5? {eom} => | 4
tom has 6 apples. ann has 8 apples. sam has 20 pens. => tom owns apples; apples is 6; 6 = 6; ann owns apples; apples is 8; 8 = 8; sam owns pens; pens is 20; 20 = 20 | what number is between 6 and 8? = 7; what number comes between 19 and 21? = 20; what number is between 100 and 98? = 99; which number is between 3 and 5? = 4; how many apples does tom have? = 6; how many apples does ann have? = 8; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 14; how many more apples does ann have than tom? = 2; how many pens does sam have? = 20; who has more apples? = ann
kim has 12 cards. joe has 14 cards. lily has 50 stamps. => kim owns cards; cards is 12; 12 = 12; joe owns cards; cards is 14; 14 = 14; lily owns stamps; stamps is 50; 50 = 50 | what number is between 12 and 14? = 13; which number is between 49 and 51? = 50; what number comes between 13 and 15? = 14; how many cards does kim have? = 12; how many cards do kim and joe have? = 26; how many more cards does joe have than kim? = 2; how many stamps does lily have? = 50; who has fewer cards? = kim; what number is between 14 and 12? = 13
