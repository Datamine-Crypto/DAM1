# My things: the speaker's own thing is told and asked with my, as his and her are for a name. A
# statement about my thing keeps what the thing is, where it is or who it is; a question about my
# thing is answered from it, and with nothing when the thing was never told.
# Each scene tells two or three of my things or my kin, then asks where each thing is, its color, what
# is in each place, yes and no, and a thing or a quality never told, which is nothing. Each turn tells
# one thing and asks for another.
i have a key. my key is in the drawer. i have a bike. my bike is blue. => i owns key; key in drawer; i owns bike; bike is blue | where is my key? = drawer; what is in the drawer? = key; what color is my bike? = blue; what is blue? = bike; is my key in the drawer? = yes; is my bike red? = no; is my key blue? = no; where is my bike? = nothing; what color is my key? = nothing; where is my phone? = nothing
