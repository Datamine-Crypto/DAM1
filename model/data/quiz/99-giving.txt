# Giving: gave the thing to someone makes that one its owner. The word gave points owns and turns
# the pointer round to wait for its subject, so the thing that follows is the subject and the one
# after to, completing it, owns the thing; the one who had it no longer does, as owning replaces.
# The word to points inward as it does in went to the kitchen, and a turned-round pointer stays.
# Each scene tells several givings of different things, then asks who has each thing, what each
# one has, whether the giver still has it, and a thing no one has.
tom has an apple. tom gave the apple to ann. mary gave the football to john. fred gave the milk to bill. => ann owns apple; john owns football; bill owns milk | who has the apple? = ann; what does ann have? = apple; who has the football? = john; what is john carrying? = football; what does bill have? = milk; who has the milk? = bill; does tom have the apple? = no; does ann have the apple? = yes; who owns the football? = john; what is bill carrying? = milk; does mary have the football? = no; who is holding the apple? = ann; does ann have the milk? = no; what does tom have? = nothing; who has the pen? = nothing
