# Telling: a question that names one thing and asks nothing else is answered with what the
# reader knows of it, each relation a short sentence, sorted. The words around the name (tell me
# about, what do you know about) add nothing.
# Each line is a scene: two or three things with two facts each, kept apart so no fact tells of
# two of them, then many questions (tell me about each, and plain questions about each fact).
tom is in the house. tom has a car. the cat is black. the cat is on the bed. => tom in house; tom owns car; cat is black; cat in bed | tell me about tom? = tom has car, tom is in house; what do you know about tom? = tom has car, tom is in house; tell me about the cat? = cat is black, cat is in bed; what do you know about the cat? = cat is black, cat is in bed; tell me about the car? = tom has car; where is tom? = house; what does tom have? = car; who has the car? = tom; what color is the cat? = black; where is the cat? = bed; tell me about the dog? = nothing
