# More and less than a number: some more than a number is the number with that many added, and some
# less than it is the number with that many taken away.
# The turns ask one number each. Each scene tells what three people have, then asks more and less than
# the numbers, each count, a total, how many more one has, and who has fewer or more.
turn: what is 10 more than 35? {eom} => | 45
turn: what is 1 less than 50? {eom} => | 49
turn: what number is 100 more than 250? {eom} => | 350
turn: what is 5 less than 12? {eom} => | 7
tom has 35 apples. ann has 10 apples. sam has 50 pens. => tom owns apples; apples is 35; 35 = 35; ann owns apples; apples is 10; 10 = 10; sam owns pens; pens is 50; 50 = 50 | what is 10 more than 35? = 45; what is 1 less than 50? = 49; how many apples does tom have? = 35; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 45; how many more apples does tom have than ann? = 25; what is 10 less than 35? = 25; what number is 10 more than 50? = 60; what is 1 more than 50? = 51; how many pens does sam have? = 50; who has fewer apples? = ann
