# Choice questions: a question that offers two answers joined by or is answered with the one the
# memory holds, as a person picks between the two things they were offered.
# Each scene tells three or four facts about different things, then offers each choice both ways
# round, asks each fact plainly, yes and no, and a thing the scene never told.
