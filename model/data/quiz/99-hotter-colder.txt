# Hotter and colder: a temperature with more degrees is hotter and one with fewer degrees is colder, so
# of two temperatures the hotter has the bigger number of degrees and the colder the smaller.
# The turns compare two temperatures each. Each scene tells what three or four people have, then compares
# those numbers as degrees in all four words, asks each count, who has more or fewer, and a total.
turn: which is hotter, 30 degrees or 10 degrees? {eom} => | 30 degrees
turn: which is colder, 5 degrees or 20 degrees? {eom} => | 5 degrees
turn: which is warmer, 12 degrees or 25 degrees? {eom} => | 25 degrees
turn: which is cooler, 40 degrees or 35 degrees? {eom} => | 35 degrees
