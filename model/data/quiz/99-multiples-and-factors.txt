# Multiples and factors: a number is a multiple of another when the other divides it with nothing left
# over, and then the other is a factor of it.
# The turns ask one multiple or factor each. Each scene tells what three people have, then asks multiples
# and factors of the numbers, each count, a total, and who has more or fewer.
turn: is 12 a multiple of 4? {eom} => | yes
turn: is 15 a multiple of 4? {eom} => | no
turn: is 3 a factor of 18? {eom} => | yes
turn: is 7 a factor of 20? {eom} => | no
tom has 12 apples. ann has 4 apples. sam has 18 pens. => tom owns apples; apples is 12; 12 = 12; ann owns apples; apples is 4; 4 = 4; sam owns pens; pens is 18; 18 = 18 | is 12 a multiple of 4? = yes; is 15 a multiple of 4? = no; is 3 a factor of 18? = yes; is 7 a factor of 20? = no; is 4 a factor of 12? = yes; is 18 a multiple of 4? = no; is 18 a multiple of 3? = yes; how many apples does tom have? = 12; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 16; how many pens does sam have? = 18; who has more apples? = tom
kim has 20 cards. joe has 7 cards. lily has 15 stamps. => kim owns cards; cards is 20; 20 = 20; joe owns cards; cards is 7; 7 = 7; lily owns stamps; stamps is 15; 15 = 15 | is 20 a multiple of 7? = no; is 5 a factor of 15? = yes; is 5 a factor of 20? = yes; is 15 a multiple of 7? = no; is 20 a multiple of 5? = yes; is 7 a factor of 15? = no; how many cards does kim have? = 20; how many cards do kim and joe have? = 27; how many stamps does lily have? = 15; who has fewer cards? = joe
