# Kin named after a copula: ann is tom's mother. The name before the apostrophe first completes
# the is (ann is tom); the apostrophe takes that join back, keeps the is waiting and makes tom the
# owner of the next thing, so the kin word ends the is and is owned: ann is mother, tom owns mother.
# Each scene tells three kin, then asks each from the owner, from the kin (whose mother is ann), yes
# and no, and kin the scene never told, which is nothing.
ann is tom's mother. ben is sue's brother. lily is max's sister. => ann is mother; tom owns mother; ben is brother; sue owns brother; lily is sister; max owns sister | who is tom's mother? = ann; who is sue's brother? = ben; who is max's sister? = lily; is ann tom's mother? = yes; is ben max's brother? = no; whose mother is ann? = tom; whose brother is ben? = sue; whose sister is lily? = max; is lily max's sister? = yes; is ann sue's mother? = no; who is sue's sister? = nothing; who is tom's father? = nothing
tom is ann's father. sam is ben's uncle. rex is tom's dog. => tom is father; ann owns father; sam is uncle; ben owns uncle; rex is dog; tom owns dog | who is ann's father? = tom; who is ben's uncle? = sam; what is tom's dog? = rex; is tom ann's father? = yes; is sam ann's uncle? = no; whose father is tom? = ann; whose uncle is sam? = ben; whose dog is rex? = tom; is rex tom's dog? = yes; who is ann's mother? = nothing; what is ben's dog? = nothing
