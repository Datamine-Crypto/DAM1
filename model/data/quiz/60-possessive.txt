# The possessive: tom's car is tom owns car, the apostrophe marking the name before it as the
# owner of the name after it, the way "his" marks a name referred to. The tokenizer splits the
# apostrophe and the s into words of their own.
# Each line is a scene: a few owners with their things, then many questions about them (the color
# or place of an owner's thing, who owns it, whose it is, yes and no, and a thing not named).
tom's car is red. ann's cat is black. sue's bike is green. => tom owns car; car is red; ann owns cat; cat is black; sue owns bike; bike is green | what color is tom's car? = red; what color is ann's cat? = black; what color is sue's bike? = green; who owns the car? = tom; who owns the bike? = sue; who owns the cat? = ann; whose car is red? = tom; whose cat is black? = ann; whose bike is green? = sue; what is red? = car; what is green? = bike; what does ann have? = cat; does tom have a car? = yes; does tom have a bike? = no; what color is the hat? = nothing
sam's ball is yellow. tom's dog is in the garden. ann's key is in the box. => sam owns ball; ball is yellow; tom owns dog; dog in garden; ann owns key; key in box | what color is sam's ball? = yellow; where is tom's dog? = garden; where is ann's key? = box; who owns the ball? = sam; who owns the dog? = tom; whose ball is yellow? = sam; whose dog is in the garden? = tom; whose key is in the box? = ann; where is the dog? = garden; what is in the box? = key; what does sam have? = ball; does ann have a key? = yes; does sam have a key? = no; where is the cat? = nothing
the cat's ball is red. the dog's bone is white. sue's hat is blue. => cat owns ball; ball is red; dog owns bone; bone is white; sue owns hat; hat is blue | what is red? = ball; what is white? = bone; what is blue? = hat; what color is the cat's ball? = red; what color is the dog's bone? = white; what color is sue's hat? = blue; what does the cat have? = ball; what does the dog have? = bone; who owns the hat? = sue; whose hat is blue? = sue; does the cat have a ball? = yes; does the dog have a ball? = no; what color is the bike? = nothing
