# The successor: the thing that comes just after another in an order is its successor, so the successor of a thing is the
# relation from the one after to the one before, as the facts of an order are told. Each scene tells two to four
# successors of one order (letters, seasons, months, days, numbers), then asks the successor of each one and yes or no
# both ways; the successors that were test lines are told only in test scenes.
