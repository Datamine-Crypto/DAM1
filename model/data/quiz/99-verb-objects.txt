# Asking for the one a deed was done to: who did ann help asks the relation helped from ann, the
# plain verb after did naming the told past form, so the answer is the other end of the relation.
# Regular verbs only, where the told form is the plain verb with ed; every plain verb has two
# learned lines so it reads with a row of its own. Each scene tells four deeds by different people,
# then asks the one each deed was done to, the one who did it, and a deed not told.
