# Earlier and later: on the same day a smaller hour comes before a bigger one, so of two times the
# earlier is the one with the smaller hour and the later the one with the bigger hour.
# Each scene tells when two or three things start, then asks which of many pairs of times is earlier or
# later (the scene's own hours among them), when each thing starts, which starts first, and a thing the
# scene never told. One turn of each kind stays a chat turn.
turn: which is earlier, 3 o'clock or 5 o'clock? {eom} => | 3 o'clock
the film starts at 3. the game starts at 5. the class starts at 11. => 3 = 3; film starts 3; 5 = 5; game starts 5; 11 = 11; class starts 11 | which is earlier, 3 o'clock or 5 o'clock? = 3 o'clock; which is later, 2 o'clock or 9 o'clock? = 9 o'clock; which is earlier, 11 o'clock or 4 o'clock? = 4 o'clock; which is later, 7 o'clock or 6 o'clock? = 7 o'clock; which is later, 3 o'clock or 5 o'clock? = 5 o'clock; which is earlier, 5 o'clock or 11 o'clock? = 5 o'clock; when does the film start? = 3; when does the class start? = 11; which starts first, the film or the game? = film; which starts first, the class or the game? = game; when does the party start? = nothing
the party starts at 9. the match starts at 2. => 9 = 9; party starts 9; 2 = 2; match starts 2 | which is later, 2 o'clock or 9 o'clock? = 9 o'clock; which is earlier, 2 o'clock or 9 o'clock? = 2 o'clock; which is earlier, 11 o'clock or 4 o'clock? = 4 o'clock; which is later, 7 o'clock or 6 o'clock? = 7 o'clock; which is later, 4 o'clock or 11 o'clock? = 11 o'clock; when does the party start? = 9; when does the match start? = 2; which starts first, the party or the match? = match; when does the film start? = nothing
