# Comparisons said outright: taller, older, faster, heavier, longer name a relation from the
# first thing to the second, as bigger already does; a superlative is a quality.
# Each line is a scene: one comparison of each kind and a superlative or two, then many questions
# (which is more, is one more than the other both ways, what is more than a thing, the superlative).
# A trace kept from a one-sentence lesson covers its own sentence; the other sentences are null.
