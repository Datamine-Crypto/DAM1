# Questions asked sideways: tell me where tom is, do you know what is in the box, can you tell
# me who has the car. The words before the question word add nothing; the question is the rest.
# Each line is a scene: a few facts, then many sideways questions about them, each fact asked
# with more than one opening, and a thing the scene does not name.
tom is in the house. the key is in the box. the cat is black. => tom in house; key in box; cat is black | tell me where tom is? = house; do you know where tom is? = house; can you tell me where tom is? = house; do you know who is in the house? = tom; tell me what is in the box? = key; do you know what is in the box? = key; do you know where the key is? = box; tell me what is black? = cat; do you know what color the cat is? = black; tell me what color the cat is? = black; tell me where the dog is? = nothing
ann has a car. tom likes ann. the dog is brown. => ann owns car; tom likes ann; dog is brown | can you tell me what ann has? = car; do you know what ann has? = car; can you tell me who has the car? = ann; do you know who has the car? = ann; tell me who likes ann? = tom; do you know who tom likes? = ann; do you know what color the dog is? = brown; can you tell me what color the dog is? = brown; tell me what is brown? = dog; tell me where the car is? = nothing
the cup is in the box. the ball is in the bag. the hat is green. => cup in box; ball in bag; hat is green | tell me where the cup is? = box; do you know where the cup is? = box; can you tell me what is in the box? = cup; tell me where the ball is? = bag; can you tell me where the ball is? = bag; do you know what is in the bag? = ball; do you know what color the hat is? = green; tell me what is green? = hat; tell me where the pen is? = nothing
