# Owning named before the thing: the apostrophe after a name makes that name the owner of the next
# thing, and the name does not wait alongside it, so a quality or a place said of the thing is said
# of the thing only. Read by form, with no row of its own to learn. Each scene tells two or three
# owned things, then asks what has each quality, where each thing is, who owns it, and yes or no.
tom's dog is big. ann's cat is black. sam's ball is red. => tom owns dog; dog is big; ann owns cat; cat is black; sam owns ball; ball is red | what is big? = dog; what is black? = cat; what is red? = ball; who owns the dog? = tom; who has the ball? = sam; whose cat is black? = ann; what does sam have? = ball; what color is the cat? = black; is the dog big? = yes; is the ball big? = no; does tom have a cat? = no; what color is the ball? = red
tom's dog is in the house. kim's car is green. max's kite is yellow. => tom owns dog; dog in house; kim owns car; car is green; max owns kite; kite is yellow | where is the dog? = house; what is green? = car; what is yellow? = kite; what is in the house? = dog; who owns the car? = kim; who has the dog? = tom; whose kite is yellow? = max; what does max have? = kite; what color is the car? = green; is the kite yellow? = yes; is the car yellow? = no; is the dog in the house? = yes
test: ben's bike is blue. lily's hat is in the box. => ben owns bike; bike is blue; lily owns hat; hat in box | what is blue? = bike; where is the hat? = box; what is in the box? = hat; who owns the bike? = ben; whose hat is in the box? = lily; what color is the bike? = blue; is the bike blue? = yes; does ben have a hat? = no; what does lily have? = hat
# After a sentence the name before the apostrophe is still a new mention and the owner, not the
# thing the sentence before was about.
the cat is black. tom's dog is big. sam is tall. ann's hat is red. => cat is black; tom owns dog; dog is big; sam is tall; ann owns hat; hat is red | what is big? = dog; what is red? = hat; what is black? = cat; who is tall? = sam; who owns the dog? = tom; whose hat is red? = ann; what does ann have? = hat; what color is the hat? = red; is sam tall? = yes; is ann tall? = no; does sam have the dog? = no; is the cat black? = yes
the box is big. sam's ball is in the box. ann is happy. tom's cat is black. => box is big; sam owns ball; ball in box; ann is happy; tom owns cat; cat is black | where is the ball? = box; what is black? = cat; what is big? = box; what is in the box? = ball; who owns the ball? = sam; whose cat is black? = tom; who is happy? = ann; is ann happy? = yes; does ann have the cat? = no; what does tom have? = cat; is the box big? = yes; is the ball big? = no
the car is red. sue's cat is small. the cup is white. kim's box is big. => car is red; sue owns cat; cat is small; cup is white; kim owns box; box is big | what is small? = cat; what is big? = box; what is red? = car; what is white? = cup; who owns the cat? = sue; who has the cat? = sue; whose box is big? = kim; what does kim have? = box; what color is the cup? = white; is the car red? = yes; is the cat big? = no; does sue have the car? = no
