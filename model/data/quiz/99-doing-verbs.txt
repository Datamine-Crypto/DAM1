# Verbs of doing as relations named by the verb, each told twice so it has a row of its own:
# a chef uses a knife is chef uses knife, asked back as what does a chef use and who uses a knife.
# Each scene tells four doers with a verb and a thing each, no two with the same verb and thing, then
# asks what each does to, who or what does each, yes and no, and a doer the scene never told. A scene
# with traced lines keeps their traces in place, with null for the untraced sentences.
