# Greater and less: of two numbers, the bigger is greater than the smaller and the smaller is less than
# the bigger, and a number is equal only to itself, whether the words or the signs say it.
# The turns compare two numbers each. Each scene tells what three people have, then compares the numbers
# in words and in signs, asks each count, who has more and fewer, and a total.

turn: is 9 less than 15? {eom} => | yes
