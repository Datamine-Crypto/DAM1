# Whose asks the owner: the question word makes an unknown the owner of the next thing, as the
# apostrophe does for a name, so the answer is the name that owns it. Read by form. Each scene tells
# three or four owned things, some of the same kind with different owners, then asks whose each one
# is, where or what color it is, yes or no, and a thing no one owns.
tom's dog is big. ann's cat is black. sam's ball is big. => tom owns dog; dog is big; ann owns cat; cat is black; sam owns ball; ball is big | whose dog is big? = tom; whose ball is big? = sam; whose cat is black? = ann; who owns the dog? = tom; what color is the cat? = black; what is black? = cat; is the ball big? = yes; is the cat big? = no; what does sam have? = ball; whose kite is red? = nothing
tom's dog is in the house. sue's cup is in the bag. max's dog is brown. => tom owns dog; dog in house; sue owns cup; cup in bag; max owns dog; dog is brown | whose dog is in the house? = tom; whose dog is brown? = max; whose cup is in the bag? = sue; where is the cup? = bag; what is in the bag? = cup; who owns the cup? = sue; is the cup in the house? = no; what color is max's dog? = brown; whose cat is brown? = nothing
ann's cat is black. tom's dog is black. kim's ball is in the garden. joe's bike is red. => ann owns cat; cat is black; tom owns dog; dog is black; kim owns ball; ball in garden; joe owns bike; bike is red | whose dog is black? = tom; whose bike is red? = joe; whose cat is black? = ann; whose ball is in the garden? = kim; where is the ball? = garden; what is red? = bike; what is in the garden? = ball; who owns the bike? = joe; is the bike black? = no; whose hat is red? = nothing
fred's book is on the table. rose's kite is green. sam's cup is white. => fred owns book; book in table; rose owns kite; kite is green; sam owns cup; cup is white | whose kite is green? = rose; whose book is on the table? = fred; whose cup is white? = sam; where is the book? = table; what is green? = kite; what is white? = cup; who owns the book? = fred; is the kite white? = no; whose dog is green? = nothing
test: ben's bike is blue. lily's hat is in the box. ben's cat is black. => ben owns bike; bike is blue; lily owns hat; hat in box; ben owns cat; cat is black | whose bike is blue? = ben; whose cat is black? = ben; whose hat is in the box? = lily; where is the hat? = box; what is blue? = bike; who owns the hat? = lily; is the cat blue? = no; whose dog is blue? = nothing
