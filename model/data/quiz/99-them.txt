# kite are one pointed set; them, in a later sentence, chooses the newest set of two or more and
# the giving moves every one of them, as it moves one thing given with it. The traced line keeps its
# text and takes many questions; each scene after it tells one or two givings of a set, then asks who
# has each thing, whether the giver still has it, and a thing no one has.
