# The far end of a chain: a chain told with one comparative answers the superlative of the opposite
# adjective at its bottom. The himalayas are higher than the alps and the alps than the pyrenees,
# so the lowest is the pyrenees; rome is older than paris and paris than london, so london is the
# youngest. The opposite pairs are high and low, old and young, tall and short, big and small.
# Each scene tells two chains with different comparatives, then asks both ends of each chain, the
# comparative both ways, yes and no, a name the scene never told and a superlative it never told.
