# Price of many: things that each cost the same cost that price once for each thing, so some things at
# a price each cost the price times how many there are. The traced line and the turns stay as single
# lessons; each scene tells the price of several things, then asks what many of them cost and each price.
turn: how much do 5 pens cost at 2 dollars each? {eom} => | 10
turn: how much do 3 books cost at 7 dollars each? {eom} => | 21
turn: how much do 4 apples cost at 25 cents each? {eom} => | 100
turn: how much do 6 cups cost at 3 dollars each? {eom} => | 18
