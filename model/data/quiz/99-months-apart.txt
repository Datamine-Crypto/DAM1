# Months apart: the months of the year come in order and wrap round, so the months until a named month
# are counted forward from this month, and some months from now is that many months on.
# The turns tell this month and ask one span each. Each scene tells this month and what two people have,
# then asks months until, months on and months ago, round the end of the year too, and the counts.
seed: 11-order
turn: the month is october. how many months until december? {eom} => | 2
turn: the month is november. how many months until february? {eom} => | 3
turn: the month is march. what month will it be in 4 months? {eom} => | july
turn: the month is january. what month was it 2 months ago? {eom} => | november
the month is october. tom has 3 apples. ann has 5 apples. => month is october; tom owns apples; apples is 3; 3 = 3; ann owns apples; apples is 5; 5 = 5 | how many months until december? = 2; what month will it be in 4 months? = february; what month was it 2 months ago? = august; how many months until january? = 3; what month will it be in 3 months? = january; what month was it 5 months ago? = may; how many apples does tom have? = 3; how many apples do tom and ann have? = 8; who has more apples? = ann
the month is march. kim has 6 cards. joe has 2 cards. => month is march; kim owns cards; cards is 6; 6 = 6; joe owns cards; cards is 2; 2 = 2 | what month will it be in 4 months? = july; how many months until june? = 3; what month was it 2 months ago? = january; how many months until december? = 9; what month was it 4 months ago? = november; what month will it be in 10 months? = january; how many cards does kim have? = 6; how many more cards does kim have than joe? = 4; who has fewer cards? = joe
