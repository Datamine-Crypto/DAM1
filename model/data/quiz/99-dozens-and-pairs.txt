# Dozens and pairs: a pair is 2 things and a dozen is 12 things, so some pairs or dozens of a thing are
# that many times 2 or 12 of it.
# The traced turns stay single lessons. Each scene tells how many things two or three people have, then
# asks many dozens and pairs, each count, how many dozens or pairs a count makes, and a person the scene
# never told.
seed: measures
turn: how many eggs are in a dozen? {eom} => | 12
turn: how many eggs are in 2 dozen? {eom} => | 24
turn: how many is half a dozen? {eom} => | 6
