# Fits in, as the bAbI size stories say it: X fits in Y puts X inside Y, so two such facts chain the
# way places do (the chocolate fits in the box, the box fits in the suitcase, so the chocolate fits
# in the suitcase). The verb points inward as went does; it is no part of the thing's name.
# Each scene tells two or three chains, then asks every link, the chain end to end, and the links
# turned round, which never fit.
